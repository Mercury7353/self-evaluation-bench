Scoring & methodology

Score is 100 × Lin's concordance correlation coefficient (CCC), measuring agreement with reference scores in level, spread, and variation. Perfect agreement is 100; opposing variation can score below zero.

Pairwise agreement (PA) measures model ordering: a correct order receives 1, a measured tie ½, and a reversal 0; reference ties are excluded. Displayed as a percentage.

Results are averaged equally across targets: 6 development and 8 sealed targets. Domain columns show the corresponding target averages; Overall averages all targets rather than the three domain columns.
